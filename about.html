About HornSphere

Built around expertise.

HornSphere is a Mogadishu-based consulting company delivering research-led advisory, research capacity development and international student recruitment, with a regional focus on Somalia, the Horn of Africa and wider East Africa.

Who we are

One company. Three principal services.

We work with institutions, professionals, students and universities. Each engagement begins with the need, then brings the right people and process around it.

Our consulting model lets clients access specialist expertise while HornSphere remains responsible for scoping, delivery and the final result.
