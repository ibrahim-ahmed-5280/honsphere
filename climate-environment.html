What this covers

Climate risk and variability, environmental conditions and change, resilience and adaptation, sustainability, and the social and spatial dimensions of environmental issues. We combine field and secondary data with spatial analysis to describe what is happening, where, and what it means for people, institutions and investment.

How we help

  • check_circleClimate and environmental baseline studies and situation analysis
  • check_circleClimate risk, vulnerability and resilience assessments
  • check_circleEnvironmental and socioeconomic analysis, including spatial mapping
  • check_circleEvidence and indicators for programme design, monitoring and evaluation
  • check_circleResearch to support policy, planning and funding proposals
Representative questions

The kind of work we can address.
